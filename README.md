# Gradient Wand

A Minecraft mod that paints gradients. Set two points, and the wand fills the run between them with
a blend of whatever blocks are in your hotbar. Can use as a line, a one-block-thick wall, or a ribbon with a variable width.

## Features

- **Shapes:** strip, wall, or ribbon (a slanted wall)
- **Gradient easing:** Linear, Back-heavy, Front-heavy, or Ends.
- **Dithering:** ordered or random, with a slide to control it.
- **Grain Support:** control the direction of logs, pillars, and other directional blocks.
- **Seven tiers of wand:** wood through netherite.
- **Two new enchantments:** Capacity raises the block cap, Stamina reduces the hunger cost. Look for a libriarian or treasure!
- **Live preview:** the selection is outlined before you commit to placing them.
- **Undo and redo:** changed your mind? Just click the button.

Press **G** (default) for the settings menu while holding the wand.
